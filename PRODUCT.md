# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Art directors, creative directors, art buyers, and hiring managers at fashion houses,
luxury groups, and creative agencies in the mode/luxe sector. Visitors with a trained
eye and a high threshold — they see dozens of portfolios a week. What stops them is
a singular gaze, genuine economy of means, and precise visual staging.

## Product Purpose

Portfolio of Jérémy Rambaud, Art Director completing an M2 at EEGP Angers. Goal: land
an art direction role or project in fashion and luxury. Success means one thing — the
person looking wants to meet Jérémy.

## Brand Personality

Elegant · editorial · measured. Restraint as stance, not absence. Every visual decision
— white space, letterform, interval — is a deliberate choice. The portfolio reads like
a printed monograph: paginated, dated, definitive.

## Anti-references

- Generic DA portfolio (Behance-template aesthetics) that could belong to anyone
- AI/tech visual tells: purple or blue gradients, glassmorphism, gradient text, pervasive
  drop shadows, neon accents, default system fonts
- Motion overdrive: effects on everything, gratuitous micro-interactions, scroll effects
  for their own sake
- "Vibe-coded" or SaaS-landing-page energy — never tech-startup
- Cargo/Squarespace-style uniform grid with no editorial decision-making

## Design Principles

1. **Staging the work, not the interface.** The portfolio presents projects; the
   interface never competes with what it shows.
2. **Monograph, not portfolio.** Every page reads like a printed page: rigorous
   typography, inhabited white space, deliberate pacing.
3. **Restraint as commitment.** Every empty space is a decision, not an absence.
   Every animation earns its place or doesn't exist.
4. **Fashion-house precision.** The same rigor applied to a brand identity applies
   here — nothing arbitrary, nothing decorative for its own sake.
5. **Trust the existing system.** Romie (local @font-face), the established palette,
   and existing typographic rules are identity decisions — they don't get
   second-guessed per iteration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA. Body text >= 4.5:1 contrast on white, full keyboard navigation, visible
focus states. Honor prefers-reduced-motion: animations reduce to crossfades or instant
transitions. No specific user needs flagged beyond the standard.
